How you can help

Every form of support matters.

Healthwise is focused on turning awareness into meaningful support for people facing serious healthcare and humanitarian needs.

02

03

Transparency

Know where your support is going.

Healthwise was created to support an established humanitarian medical organization rather than attempting to provide medical services independently.

The fundraiser is organized to support Médecins Sans Frontières (Doctors Without Borders) Canada. MSF determines where its resources are needed most based on its humanitarian and medical priorities.

The goal is to make the purpose of this fundraiser clear so donors understand what they are supporting.

Step 01

Support the fundraiser

Make a donation through the official Healthwise fundraiser.

Step 02

Funds support MSF

The fundraiser is intended to support Médecins Sans Frontières (Doctors Without Borders) Canada.

Step 03

Medical humanitarian work

MSF uses its resources to provide medical humanitarian assistance where it is needed.
