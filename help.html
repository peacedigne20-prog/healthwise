Your support can help.

There are several ways to support Healthwise and the people affected by the issues we focus on.

There is more than one way to help.

You don't need to make a large contribution to have an impact. Every form of support can help bring more attention to healthcare access and humanitarian needs.

Donate

Financial support can help trusted organizations provide medical care, essential medicine, emergency supplies, and humanitarian assistance to people who need it. Even a small donation can contribute to larger efforts when many people come together.

Share

Sharing Healthwise with friends, family, and your community helps more people understand the problems surrounding healthcare access and humanitarian emergencies.

Get Involved

If you have ideas, useful information, or ways to contribute to Healthwise, getting involved can help the project reach more people and make a greater difference.

Support the cause.

Donations can help established organizations continue providing healthcare, medicine, emergency assistance, and other essential services to communities in need.

Transparency matters.

Healthwise is committed to being clear about how donations are handled. Contributions should go toward established organizations whose work directly supports people affected by healthcare shortages and humanitarian emergencies.
