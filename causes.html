The issues

Healthcare should not depend on where you live.

Healthwise focuses on healthcare access, medicine, and humanitarian emergencies affecting people around the world.

What we focus on

Three areas at the center of Healthwise.

Millions of people face barriers to basic medical care because of poverty, conflict, displacement, disasters, or limited healthcare resources. Healthwise aims to learn about these problems and support organizations working directly with affected communities.

01

Healthcare Access

Many people around the world cannot reliably access doctors, hospitals, medicines, or other essential healthcare. Healthwise raises awareness about these barriers and why access to healthcare matters.

02

Medicine

Medical supplies, treatments, vaccinations, and emergency care can become difficult to access during crises. Supporting humanitarian medical organizations can help provide care where healthcare systems are under serious pressure.

03

Humanitarian Emergencies

Conflict, displacement, disasters, and disease outbreaks can leave communities with urgent medical needs. Healthwise supports humanitarian efforts focused on providing medical care to people affected by these emergencies.

Healthcare needs exist across many regions.

Why it matters

A medical emergency can become a humanitarian emergency.

When healthcare systems are damaged or overwhelmed, people can lose access to basic treatment, medicines, vaccinations, and emergency care.

Humanitarian medical organizations work to fill some of these gaps by providing healthcare directly to people affected by conflict, disasters, disease outbreaks, and exclusion from healthcare.

Healthwise exists to help people understand these issues and to support organizations that already have the experience and infrastructure to respond.
