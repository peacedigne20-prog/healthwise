OUR MISSION

Healthcare should be available to everyone.

Healthwise is a student-led initiative focused on healthcare access and humanitarian medical needs.

WHY HEALTHWISE EXISTS

Where you are born should not decide whether you receive care.

Millions of people face barriers to healthcare because of conflict, poverty, displacement, disasters and limited medical resources.


Healthwise was created to learn more about these issues, raise awareness and support organizations that provide medical assistance to people who need it.

Photo: Alaine Knipes / CDC Global Health · CC BY 2.0
WHAT WE BELIEVE

Three things guide Healthwise.

01

Access

Everyone deserves access to essential healthcare, regardless of their circumstances or location.

02

Awareness

Understanding healthcare and humanitarian problems is an important part of creating meaningful support.

03

Action

Awareness matters, but supporting organizations that are already providing medical assistance can create direct impact.
