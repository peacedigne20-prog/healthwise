WHY WE EXIST

Access to healthcare is a basic human need.

Conflict, displacement, poverty, disasters and damaged healthcare systems can leave people without the medical care they need. Healthwise exists to raise awareness and support organizations providing medical assistance to people facing these circumstances.

Photo: Alaine Knipes / CDC Global Health · CC BY 2.0
WHAT WE FOCUS ON

Healthcare. Humanitarian aid. Access.

Healthwise focuses on people who face serious barriers to healthcare, particularly during humanitarian emergencies.

Healthcare Access

Supporting efforts that help people receive essential medical care, medicine and health services.

Humanitarian Emergencies

Supporting medical humanitarian work in communities affected by conflict, displacement and disasters.

Essential Medicine

Medical care depends on medicines, supplies, healthcare workers and functioning medical facilities.
